## Research Projects

### 01. When Public Transportation Depends on a Phone
**An Ethical Analysis of Metra and the Ventra App**

*Technology Ethics | Public Transportation | Digital Accessibility*

This paper examines the ethical implications of smartphone-dependent public transportation systems, using Metra and the Ventra app as a case study. It explores digital accessibility, transportation equity, and the responsibilities of transit agencies when implementing new technologies.

**Key Topics:** Digital inequality, public transportation, technology ethics, accessibility

**Course:** Ethics in Technology | June 2026
